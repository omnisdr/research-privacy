OmniSDR Research Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

OmniSDR Research is an internal research tool run by OmniSDR. It is not a public app and has no sign-up. It does not post, message, comment or change anything on Instagram or Facebook.

What it reads

Using Meta's Instagram Business Discovery feature, it looks up public information on Instagram business and creator accounts: the account's username, follower count, number of posts, and counts of likes and comments on recent posts.

What it stores

Counts and the username only. It does not store post content, captions, images, videos or comment text.

Why

To judge, from public numbers, whether an online business may be a good fit for OmniSDR's services, so that OmniSDR can decide whether to contact that business. Any contact is made using facts the business itself publishes.

Who sees it

Only OmniSDR staff. We do not sell, license, share or export this data, and we do not use it to build profiles of individual people.

How long we keep it

Until the business is dropped from our research list or the data is no longer needed, then it is deleted.

Deleting your data

If you own an Instagram account and want any data we hold about it removed, email support@omnisdr.com with the account's username. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm. Meta can also instruct us to delete data, and we will.

Contact

OmniSDR, support@omnisdr.com
